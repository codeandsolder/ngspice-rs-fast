Baking reduces retired instructions by 4.5%, but cycles by only 1.5%. Device dispatch unrolling alone is not a significant speedup and slows the 100-diode operating point. The specialized executable is 7494664 bytes versus 6232576 bytes for the initial generic-only build; this includes the generated schedules, emitter and benchmark machinery. No conclusion about fully specialized sparse factorization or in-process JIT follows from these results.
